
# From Papers to Problems: Preparing Computing Students for Interdisciplinary AI for Science

This repository contains teaching activities and instructional resources accompanying our paper, *From Papers to Problems: Preparing Computing Students for Interdisciplinary AI for Science*, accepted to the ACM Technical Symposium on Computer Science Education (SIGCSE TS 2027).

## Teaching Materials

The repository includes resources developed or adapted for an interdisciplinary graduate-level AI4Science course at the University of Michigan.

- **Jigsaw Activity:** Instructions for collaborative learning activities in which students explore research papers and share their understanding with peers.
- **Case Study:** Adapted Requirements Table and Statement of Work resources to guide students in formulating interdisciplinary scientific problems.
- **Presentation Guide:** Guidance for student presentations.

## Paper

**From Papers to Problems: Preparing Computing Students for Interdisciplinary AI for Science**

**Authors:** Diana Guadalupe Gomez and Alexander Rodríguez  
**Conference:** SIGCSE TS 2027  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1145/3857223.3863146
